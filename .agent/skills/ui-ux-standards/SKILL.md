## Rules
- **Whitespace**: Maintain generous padding/margins. Do not cram elements together.
- **Hierarchy**: Use clear typography scale (`text-h1` to `text-caption`) to establish order.
- **Interaction**: Ensure all interactive states (hover, focus, active, disabled) are distinct and visible.
