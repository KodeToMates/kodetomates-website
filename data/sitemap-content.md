## 17. Enterprise Content Standards
- Keep messaging clear and human; avoid empty corporate jargon.
- Lead with user value and outcomes rather than features.
- Use specific claims only when KodeToMates can support them.
- Use one primary CTA per major section.
- Keep terminology consistent across pages.
- Design copy for scanning: short paragraphs, headings, bullets and clear actions.
- Every project should explain the problem, solution, technology and current status.
- Future pages should fit the same Learn → Build → Collaborate → Grow ecosystem.
