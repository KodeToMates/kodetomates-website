Define a token-first approach.

Use CSS variables/design tokens for:

- colors
- spacing
- typography
- radius
- shadows
- motion
- breakpoints

KodeToMates brand foundation:

Deep Forest:
#17352B

Kode Green:
#55745F

Warm Cream:
#F7F1E7

Warm Sand:
#D8B88C

Accent colors may be introduced selectively.

Do NOT introduce new colors randomly.

Components should consume tokens instead of repeatedly
hardcoding values.
